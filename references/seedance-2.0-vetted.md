# Seedance 2.0: route boundary and shared shot methods

Read this when Seedance 2.0 is selected. It does not establish which engine Cinema Studio 3.5 uses. Comparing a general filming method across models also does not transfer a model's inputs or controls to another route.

## Dated routing evidence

The official CLI Skill 0.13.0 reviewed for this project listed Seedance 2.0 as a candidate for native 4K work and generally favored Seedance 2.5 for other serious video requests. This is a recorded recommendation from that release, not a current capability check or a quality guarantee. Its provenance is recorded in [sources.md](sources.md). Confirm a required output option in the selected route before recommending it as available; follow [platform-access.md](platform-access.md) for permitted read-only checks.

## Apply the film's existing decisions

Use [prompt-and-shot-guide.md](prompt-and-shot-guide.md) to determine what belongs in one generation, how much time the approved action needs, which references are sufficient and how it connects to the edit. Use [prompt-delivery.md](prompt-delivery.md) to turn that decision into a complete English prompt and a faithful Chinese translation. Neither file depends on a vendor prompt package.

For a continuous take, preserve the approved space and one understandable camera route throughout the action. For an intentional edit sequence, make the cuts legible and preserve the relevant state at each boundary. The method is chosen from the director's shot, not from a mandatory model-specific ordering of prompt sections. Performance, physical interactions and sound receive detail only where the scene needs it.

A model label does not establish a reference slot, resolution, voice control or Studio preset. Keep verified settings outside the prompt and describe their needed visible consequences inside it under the shared delivery rules. Do not add numerical precision, unsupported match guarantees or output-resolution slogans to compensate for missing evidence. The director retains control of page binding and generation.
