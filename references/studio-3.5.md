## Best fit

Choose 3.5 for a short concentrated beat with one dominant action or emotional change, especially when the adjacent shots already use it and its visible controls express the intended camera language. A group can behave as one controllable subject when everyone follows one cue, one motion, and one held end state.

Do not choose it merely because an action is fast. Exact group geometry, several independent contacts, multiple injuries, and a moving camera can still overload one 15-second generation.

## Selection meaning

- 35mm + f/4 character: grounded action, readable geography, and practical focus tolerance.
- 50mm + f/4 character: natural dialogue and medium close coverage.
- 75mm + f/1.4 character: isolated facial emotion; poor for a moving group.
- 14mm/8mm: scale and aggressive proximity, with edge and face distortion risk.
- Classic Static: exact composition, tension, and VFX-friendly blocking.
- One Take: continuous geography with a simple causal chain.
- Intimate Observer: restrained body/face performance.
- Documentary Snap: observational urgency; use only when count and geometry are already secure.
- Raw Chaos: deliberate disorder; avoid when the main problem is formation or contact accuracy.
- Window, Soft Cross, Practicals: motivated and readable faces.
- Contre-jour or Silhouette: threat and outline at the cost of facial detail.

Auto or a neutral choice is often the most precise option when approved references already own color, light, or geography. A preset is not a substitute for first-frame blocking.

## Group-action rule

For an exact six-person tactical advance, the formation reference must own count and geometry. Choose one camera grammar:

- One Take for a continuous advance, hand signal, synchronized stop, and held end state;
- Documentary Snap for short observational inserts after the geometry is already established;
- never stack a chaotic movement language with exact formation, several scanning directions, and multiple contacts.

This is conditional cinematography guidance, not a fixed military preset.
