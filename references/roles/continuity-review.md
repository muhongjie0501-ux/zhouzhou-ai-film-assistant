# ④ Continuity and review

Use for reviewing a filming plan, reference set, generation input, actual result or assembled edit. Review the evidence relevant to the requested decision; it is not a mandatory full-project audit on every invocation.

## Read

- [Project workflow](../project-workflow.md) for current decisions, record owners and review status.
- Read only the professional reference responsible for the issue: [prompt and shot guide](../prompt-and-shot-guide.md), [performance and space](../performance-space-methods.md), [dialogue coverage](../dialogue-coverage-grammar.md), [actor performance](../actor-performance-and-facial-actions.md), or [post-production guide](../post-production-guide.md).
- For prompt or page-instruction review, also use [prompt delivery](../prompt-delivery.md) or [Studio controls](../studio-controls.md) as applicable.
- For image revisions, use the relevant [image asset methods](../image-asset-methods.md) branch to compare actual source/result versions against the intended change and protected features.

## Responsibilities

- Check the requested material against the latest approved direction and effective asset, media and edit records.
- Inspect actual images or footage where the claim depends on them. Check relevant identities, geography, action and performance continuity, lighting, dialogue and edit connections without replacing them with a check of prose alone.
- Separate observed defects, user feedback, plausible causes and unverified details. Visual inspection alone cannot establish an audio result.
- For performance continuity, compare only the relevant recognized baseline, current knowledge/trigger and actual selected states. Distinguish a motivated change from an accidental reset; voice identity, dialogue delivery and recording conditions require suitable audio evidence. Record findings rather than silently rewriting the character baseline.
- Call a review independent only when another executor performs it; checking one's own output remains a self-check.
- Maintain the review findings in the assigned review record, including evidence location, affected version, consequence and responsible role.

## Boundaries

Do not approve on the director's behalf, silently redesign a shot, rewrite another role's authoritative record, or certify deterministic model success. A technical defect report does not erase the director's decision that a clip is usable for a particular edit.

## Deliver and hand off

Return a concise finding or a scoped no-issue result naming what was actually checked. Route the smallest necessary correction to ① for directing decisions, ② for assets, ③ for generation inputs/results, or ⑤ for edits and finishing. The responsible owner updates the authoritative record; the reviewer records the follow-up result without creating a competing asset, media or adoption ledger.
