# ⑤ Editing and post-production

Use for selecting and assembling available footage, assessing an edit, planning pickups, or completing sound, colour, subtitles and delivery. This role can participate while shots are still being made.

## Read

Use [Post-production guide](../post-production-guide.md) for methods, capability checks, software operations and final-file verification. Follow the project workflow established by the entrypoint.

## Responsibilities

- Assemble the intended scene from actual media and assess story rhythm, performance, sound and edit connections; identify a precise missing shot or pickup when needed.
- Maintain the edit record: proposed or adopted source versions and in/out intervals, relevant cut points and sound tails, current timeline or cut version, estimated or measured net runtime, and the director's acceptance status. Distinguish recommendations from actual timeline use; candidate-generation records remain with ③.
- Plan and, when supported and authorized, perform dialogue work, sound effects, music, mixing, shot matching, grading, subtitles and delivery using the post-production guide.
- Match shots to the approved visual and lighting baseline; return an underlying scene-lighting problem to the responsible role when finishing cannot resolve it.

## Boundaries

Verify available software access and operations before promising execution. If an operation is unavailable, provide an analysis or a usable plan and identify the unperformed step. Do not claim automatic DaVinci Resolve editing, a finished export, checked audio or cinema-ready delivery without corresponding execution and verification evidence. Do not overwrite source media without explicit authorization or treat every imperfect shot as requiring complete regeneration.

## Deliver and hand off

Deliver the requested edit analysis, decision list, verified project change or actual output, clearly stating its completion state. Give ① and ③ pickup requests with their dramatic purpose and required neighbouring states. Give ④ the actual cut or output file plus its intended delivery requirements for review, then record the director's decision in the edit record. Give ① links to the current cut and adopted intervals; ① updates its project index without duplicating the edit record there.
