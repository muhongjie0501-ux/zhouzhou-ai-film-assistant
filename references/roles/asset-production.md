# ② Asset production

Use for character, wardrobe, prop, location or composition references, including their image prompts and review of the resulting pictures.

- [Prompt delivery](../prompt-delivery.md) for image-prompt language, translation and Element syntax.

- [Performance and space methods](../performance-space-methods.md), and [dialogue coverage](../dialogue-coverage-grammar.md) when a requested angle needs them.

## Responsibilities

- Start from ①'s approved visual and staging requirements; inspect the relevant actual pictures before judging their suitability.
- Prepare or revise only the assets needed for those requirements. Apply the shared distinction between identity-reference lighting and scene-composition lighting.
- For a revision, identify the source version, intended change and critical preservation scope; pass those to ④ with the actual result. Asset poses support the approved shot, but do not redefine the character's behavior/voice baseline or determine a video performance by themselves.
- Maintain the image prompts and asset record: exact name, platform or supplied-file reference, effective version, intended use, review state and material uncertainties. Distinguish an image that exists from a saved Element and from an asset bound on the current generation page.
- Keep platform images there and inspect them through available read-only access under the shared storage policy. Do not save another local reference-image copy or automatically download one as a fallback; this is not a promise that the UI or tools create no temporary cache.

## Boundaries

Do not redesign the approved action or camera coverage to accommodate a convenient picture. Do not mandate a new still for every shot or a fixed multi-view pack. Follow the manual-generation boundary: the director prepares, saves and approves new assets. Planned assets must remain labelled as planned.

## Deliver and hand off

For a missing or changed asset, deliver its purpose, saved name and one bilingual image prompt using the prompt-delivery guide. After actual review, hand ③ the effective asset reference and what is confirmed, unsuitable or still unknown; report any required staging change to ①. Give ④ the actual pictures and relevant record versions for review. Keep acceptance separate from the assistant's suitability assessment.
