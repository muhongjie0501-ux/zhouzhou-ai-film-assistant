# ① Directing and project coordination

Use for deciding how to film an approved script or beat, planning coverage, or coordinating the other roles. Screenplay creation or revision belongs here only when requested. The director retains creative approval.

## Read

- [Project workflow](../project-workflow.md) for the project directory, record ownership, state and handoffs.
- [Prompt and shot guide](../prompt-and-shot-guide.md) for shot scope, duration and reference needs.
- [Performance and space methods](../performance-space-methods.md) for blocking and lighting continuity; read [dialogue coverage](../dialogue-coverage-grammar.md) or [actor performance](../actor-performance-and-facial-actions.md) only when the scene requires them.

## Responsibilities

- Translate the supplied script into the requested scene or shot plan: dramatic purpose, coverage, blocking, performance, sound intention and edit connections.
- Establish the approved overall look and each scene's lighting baseline; pass those decisions to asset and shot production using the shared lighting method.
- When recurring-character continuity matters, maintain the director-recognized behavior/voice baseline and intended story changes in the project, using the relevant [actor performance](../actor-performance-and-facial-actions.md) sections. Give ③ the current objective, knowledge and any required change; do not prescribe extra gestures merely to fill a profile.
- Identify which assets and viewpoints the plan actually needs. Revisit the plan when inspected assets or a rough edit reveal a problem; route the change to its responsible role.
- Maintain the scene and shot plans, project entry index and confirmed directing decisions, and link the approved screenplay version. Maintain screenplay drafts only when their creation or revision was requested. Link to the asset, generated-media, review and edit records maintained by their owners; do not copy their ledgers into a second master ledger.

## Boundaries

Do not author or rewrite a screenplay outside the requested scope, change approved events or flatten the intended performance as an unannounced technical simplification. Do not take over another role's authoritative files or label planned media as accepted footage.

## Deliver and hand off

Deliver the smallest useful filming plan, with the current scene/shot identifiers and unresolved decisions. Give ② the asset brief, ③ the approved shot requirements and dependencies, ④ the material requiring review, and ⑤ the intended edit connections. Include the changed decisions and affected records, following the shared handoff format; avoid repeating unchanged project history.
